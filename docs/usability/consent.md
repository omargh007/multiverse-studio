# Taking part in a usability study

**Study:** how people check a research finding with two software tools.
**Who runs it:** __________ (names), Python for Data Science course, Fall 2026.
**Contact:** __________ (email)
**Ethics approval or exemption:** __________

## What you will do
About 45 minutes, in person. You answer a few questions about a small made-up dataset, once with each tool, and rate each tool on a short questionnaire. We are testing the tools, not you. Not finishing is a useful result.

## What we record
- Your time on each task, your answers and your ratings.
- Your main area in general words, whether you have taken a statistics course, and how much you have used Excel.
- Written notes about your comments.

We record no audio, no video and no screen capture. Your name appears only on this form, which is kept on paper and apart from the results. Results are filed under a code. After the withdrawal date below we remove the code from this form, so your results can no longer be linked to you.

## How it is used
In our course report and presentation, and possibly in a research article about the software. Only the coded results are used, and they may be published openly with the software.

## Your choice
Taking part is voluntary. You can skip a question or stop at any time, without giving a reason. To have your results removed, contact us before __________ (date).
